# {{TITLE}}: style guide

> Write this from the reference (frames, video or image library) before the shot list.
> Take the grammar of the reference, never its content, logos or characters.

## Palette (hex)
- Background: #141413
- Ink: #F0EEE6
- Dim: #8A887F
- Accent (one): #D97757

## Type
- Display: Source Serif 4, 700, tracking -0.02em. Big moments only.
- UI: Inter 500/700, tracking -0.01em. Labels, UI copy, captions.
- Code: JetBrains Mono 400.

## Pacing
- Shot length: 2 to 4 s (4 to 8 beats at 120 BPM).
- Transitions: <match cut / mask wipe / morph / push>. No crossfades.
- How text enters and exits: <rises out of a mask slot on a heavy spring; leaves before the next move>.

## Camera
- <locked | slow push | handheld drift via noise()>. Moves land on downbeats.

## Texture
- <flat | paper grain | film grain>. No glows on UI, no gradients on chrome.

## Banned for this film
- Centered title on gradient, everything fading in, corner labels, frame borders, particle bursts.
