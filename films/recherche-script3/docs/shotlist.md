# Script 3 · L'ethnographie institutionnelle (2:39, 9:16 + 16:9)

One object carries the film: **the frame (le cadre)**. Institutions see people through their texts (forms,
rules, policies) and those texts draw a frame that some people don't fit. Institutional ethnography
starts from the person, follows the texts, and redraws the frame. Documents are paper (light),
institutions are blue, lived experience is amber.
